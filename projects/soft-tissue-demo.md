# SOFT TISSUE — Client / Investor Demo V1

## Overview

SOFT TISSUE is a Flutter-based B2B tissue-product ordering and business workflow prototype created as a client/investor demonstration for a tissue-paper manufacturing business.

The prototype demonstrates the complete journey from a customer placing an order to the business receiving, managing, and completing that order.

> This repository documentation describes the prototype/demo version. It is not the production backend or final commercial application.

## Customer Workflow

```text
Home
  ↓
Products
  ↓
Product Details
  ↓
Cart
  ↓
Customer Details
  ↓
Submit Order
  ↓
Order Confirmation
```
